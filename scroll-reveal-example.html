Word-by-Word Reveal

Scroll-driven pin & reveal text effect - one word at a time

Section One

"The journey of a thousand miles begins with one step." - Lao Tzu

This is the first section that demonstrates the scroll-driven pin & reveal text effect. As you scroll, the text will gradually become more visible, starting from a low opacity and transitioning to full visibility.

The effect creates an engaging reading experience where content reveals itself in proportion to the user's scroll progress through each section.

Section Two

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." - Leonardo da Vinci

Each section is designed to take up at least the full viewport height, ensuring that the pinning effect has enough space to work properly. The text remains centered in the viewport during the reveal animation.

This creates a smooth, cinematic experience that draws attention to the content and encourages users to engage with the text at their own pace.

Section Three

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works." - Steve Jobs

The implementation uses vanilla JavaScript with performance optimizations like requestAnimationFrame and throttling to ensure smooth animations even on lower-end devices.

CSS containment and will-change properties help the browser optimize the rendering performance for these animations.

Section Four

"The best way to predict the future is to invent it." - Alan Kay

This effect can be easily adapted for use in modern frameworks like React, Vue, or Angular by extracting the scroll logic into custom hooks or composables.

The modular approach allows for easy customization of timing, colors, and animation curves to match your design requirements.

Section Five

"Less is more." - Ludwig Mies van der Rohe

The effect gracefully degrades on mobile devices and smaller screens, ensuring a good user experience across all devices.

By using CSS sticky positioning and JavaScript for progress calculation, we achieve smooth animations without expensive layout thrashing.

"In the end, simplicity wins."
